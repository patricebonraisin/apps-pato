Privacy Policy

Pulse was designed for simple play, with no ads or advertising tracking.

Last updated: August 25, 2026

THE ESSENTIALS

✓

Your data stays limited

Pulse does not collect profile data, location data, advertising identifiers, or usage data for analytics or advertising purposes.

DATA USED BY PULSE

Scores and preferences

Pulse stores your best scores, game statistics, daily challenge progress, sound and vibration preferences, and tutorial status. This information enables the game to work and saves your progress.

iCloud sync

When you are signed in to iCloud, some game data and preferences are synced through Apple's iCloud key-value store, so they can be available on devices associated with the same Apple Account.

Game Center

If you choose to sign in to Game Center, Pulse submits your best score to Game Center for its leaderboards. Apple manages your Game Center account and related data in accordance with its own privacy policy.

WHAT PULSE DOES NOT DO

No commercial tracking

  • No advertising or in-app purchases.
  • No third-party analytics or marketing trackers.
  • No collection of your location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera data.
  • No sale or sharing of your data for advertising purposes.

YOUR CHOICES

Managing your data

You can turn off Game Center in your device settings. You can also delete Pulse data by deleting the app and, if needed, manage its iCloud data in your Apple Account settings. Game Center data is managed through Apple services.
