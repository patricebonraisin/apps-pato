PLAYING PULSE

How do I score points?

Tap the screen when the colored circle reaches the glowing target. The more precise your timing, the more your combo and score increase.

Which mode should I choose?

Classic mode is the main challenge. Zen mode lets you practice without ending the game when you miss. In Time Attack, you have 60 seconds to score as many points as possible. The Daily Challenge gives every player the same sequence, with one attempt per day.

Where can I find my records?

From the home screen, open Statistics to view your best scores. If Game Center is connected, you can also compare your performance on the leaderboards.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Sound or vibrations aren't working.

First, check Pulse's settings. Then make sure Silent Mode, your device volume, and the system accessibility settings are not disabling these feedback features.

My score isn't syncing with Game Center.

Sign in to Game Center in iOS Settings, then relaunch Pulse. An internet connection may be required to submit scores to the leaderboards.

How do I restart the tutorial?

From the home screen, open Settings, then choose the option to view the tutorial again.
